# Office Hours — ClaimLens

**Mode:** startup diagnostic. **Status:** evidence-limited design decision.

## Six forcing answers

1. **Demand reality:** sellers already pay 10–25% contingency for single-channel recovery, and public complaints establish fee pain. This proves category spend, not demand for ClaimLens. The next proof must be a paid or contingency-authorized audit from a real multichannel seller.
2. **Status quo:** an operator downloads settlement, order, refund, inventory, and MCF exports; joins them in spreadsheets; opens support cases; then pays a channel specialist. The cost is missed 60-day windows, analyst hours, and 10–25% of recovered cash.
3. **Desperate customer:** the finance/ops lead at a $2M–$20M GMV brand whose TikTok or Shopify order was fulfilled by FBA and whose books no longer explain the payout. Their month-end close and cash forecast are wrong; a claim window is expiring.
4. **Narrowest paid wedge:** a human-verified 60-day audit of Amazon-fulfilled off-Amazon orders, producing a ranked discrepancy ledger and one claim-ready evidence packet. Do not begin with five live connectors or autonomous filing.
5. **Observation plan:** recruit five seller operators through agencies and r/FulfillmentByAmazon; watch each reconcile one real exception without guidance; measure exports needed, time-to-explanation, false positives, evidence gaps, and whether they authorize a contingency claim.
6. **Future-fit:** marketplace fragmentation increases reconciliation joins, but platform automation shrinks easy recovery cases. ClaimLens survives only if it owns cross-channel evidence lineage and ambiguous exceptions—not commodity Amazon warehouse-loss detection.

## Premise challenge

- The real product is not “find refunds”; it is **prove where money broke across systems**.
- A broad multichannel platform before one paid audit is a trust-heavy platform fantasy.
- Doing nothing is rational if a seller has one channel or <$2M GMV; qualification is part of the product.

## Approaches considered

1. **Concierge CSV audit (recommended, S / low risk):** deterministic imports, exception scoring, exportable evidence packet. Fastest demand proof.
2. **Read-only Amazon + TikTok connectors (L / high risk):** fresher data but authorization, schema drift, and trust slow learning.
3. **Autonomous recovery network (XL / very high risk):** ideal architecture eventually; premature before channel-specific case yield and permissions are known.

## Assignment

Obtain one anonymized 60-day export bundle from a qualified multichannel seller and ask for written authorization to charge 20% only if the audit finds and recovers money.
