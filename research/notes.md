# Source notes — ClaimLens

- **Report:** “Missing marketplace money recovery for multichannel sellers,” Oct 12, 2026
- **Public URL:** https://www.ideabrowser.com/hub/ideas/today
- **IdeaBrowser index:** 6.8; **potential:** $3M–$6M ARR; **difficulty:** moderate; **capital:** $10K–$30K; **first revenue:** 60–90 days.
- **Buyer:** finance/operations lead at a $2M–$20M GMV brand selling through Amazon plus TikTok Shop, Walmart, Shopify, or eBay.
- **Painful status quo:** marketplace exports, 60-day claim windows, Seller Central cases, payout spreadsheets, and channel-specific vendors. Money can disappear between sale, fulfillment, return, and reimbursement.
- **Wedge/gap:** an order-level, cross-marketplace ledger that explains one discrepancy and prepares its evidence packet. Existing recovery firms focus on Amazon or Walmart; reconciliation tools post journals but generally do not file claims.
- **Why now:** Amazon announced broader multichannel account connections on Sep 24, 2026; 95%+ of its sellers reportedly sell on multiple channels. FBA reimbursement valuation changed to manufacturing cost in March 2025, and shorter claim windows make frequent reconciliation necessary. US TikTok Shop GMV reportedly reached $11.8B in H1 2026, +103% YoY.
- **Demand signals:** Getida says discrepancies can equal 1–3% of FBA revenue and cites a brand with $4.4M recovered since Q4 2021. Public seller posts complain about 25% contingency fees and describe long claim fights. YouTube reimbursement tutorials show thousands to ~19K views. These are directional; no ClaimLens interviews, deposits, or transaction evidence exist.
- **Competitors:** Getida (25% contingency), Seller Investigators (25%), TrueOps (10%), Helium 10 (10–15% plus subscription), Threecolts Seller 365/Margin Pro, RefundPros, and A2X ($29+/month reconciliation without claims). The report maps 10 players.
- **Offer ladder:** free 60-day cross-channel audit; first $1,000 recovered free; managed recovery at 20% Amazon / 25% other channels; $149/month payout monitoring. Prototype tests the audit artifact, not autonomous filing.
- **GTM:** no meaningful search volume for cross-channel buyer language; Amazon reimbursement and competitor terms have modest reported search volume (320–390/month). Initial motion is targeted outbound, Amazon seller communities, agency partners, and Getida displacement—not SEO.
- **Economics:** report models a $5M seller × 2% recoverable × 22% blended fee = $22K annual revenue. Reply rate, close rate, retention, and year-one $90K–$140K revenue are assumptions, not proof.
- **Biggest risks:** Amazon automation/policy may shrink the pool; Walmart specialists already exist; read-only access across five marketplaces creates a SOC 2/reference-customer trust hurdle; cold outbound is founder-intensive; false positives can waste claim windows or damage account trust.
- **Build prompts:** phase 1 CSV reconciliation and evidence packet; phase 2 read-only connectors and case tracking; phase 3 human-approved claim filing; phase 4 monitored multichannel ledger. Never auto-file until channel rules, permissions, and audit trails are proven.
